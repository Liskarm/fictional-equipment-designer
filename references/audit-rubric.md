# Fictional Equipment Audit Rubric

Run this audit before final delivery. Judge against the selected fidelity dial and medium, not against unstated real-world assumptions.

## Scoring

Score each dimension:

- **0 — Missing or contradictory:** the design cannot currently support the claim.
- **1 — Present but fragile:** usable, with an important gap or unsupported assumption.
- **2 — Coherent and evidenced:** clear, connected to other dimensions, and appropriate for the medium.

| Dimension | 0–2 test |
|---|---|
| Thesis | Can the operator, task, principle, advantage, and price be stated in one sentence? |
| Distinct role | Does it create a choice rather than duplicate an existing item or add raw power? |
| Mechanism | Does each signature capability have an enabler, input, control, consequence, limit, failure, and counter? |
| Operator loop | Can the complete preparation–use–reset–maintenance loop be described? |
| Resource and logistics | Are capacity, recovery, supply, production, and service burdens visible? |
| Counterplay | Can opponents, environment, institutions, or users meaningfully respond? |
| Visual coherence | Do silhouette, materials, state cues, and faction language follow function and setting? |
| Lifecycle and variants | Do history and variants express changed needs and meaningful tradeoffs? |
| World consequences | Are impacts proportional to rarity, replicability, and reach, with winners and losers? |
| Canon discipline | Are facts, user constraints, inferences, proposals, unknowns, sources, and conflicts separated? |

Interpretation:

- **17–20:** ready for delivery; retain declared unknowns.
- **13–16:** workable draft; repair the lowest dimensions or explicitly defer them.
- **8–12:** concept only; do not present as a complete dossier.
- **0–7:** reframe the premise before adding detail.

Do not expose the numeric score unless it helps the user; use it internally to drive revision.

## Automatic blockers

Fix or disclose these even when the total score is high:

- a capability without a cost, limit, or counter;
- a resource or power system that would transform the world but is treated as item-only without explanation;
- operator interaction that contradicts anatomy, costume, armor, scale, or depicted views;
- faction incapable of producing or maintaining the design without an acknowledged external dependency;
- variant that strictly replaces every previous model without scarcity or other reason for coexistence;
- visual ornament presented as a functional mechanism with no connection to the design ledger;
- exact performance or canon claim without a source or explicit project rule;
- unsupported retcon or silent resolution of conflicting canon;
- generated art that contradicts the approved brief;
- actionable real-world weapon construction or optimization detail.

## Cross-check procedure

1. Compare the thesis against every section; remove features that do not serve it.
2. Compare mechanism state changes against visual cues and operator actions.
3. Compare tactical advantages against counters, resources, and failure recovery.
4. Compare production claims against materials, finish, rarity, and variant availability.
5. Compare technology reach against social and institutional consequences.
6. Compare every canon claim against its label and source.
7. Compare every generated or proposed visual detail against the written brief.
8. Remove false precision and any build-enabling real-world detail.

## Final uncertainty note

End a full dossier with three compact lists when applicable:

- **Established:** facts and user constraints the design relies on.
- **Proposed:** creative decisions awaiting approval.
- **Open:** unknowns that would materially change the next iteration.
